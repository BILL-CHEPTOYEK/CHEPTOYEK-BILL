Open with a concrete moment or a specific claim, not a throat-clear ("In today's world..."). The reader should know your angle by the end of paragraph one.

## Section named for its idea, not its function

Every section should say something that would be false if you swapped the topic for a different one. Use a real detail from something you actually built or broke - a specific script, a specific failure, a specific number. Generic advice is the thing to avoid; this site's bar is a distinct voice over safe, predictable "blog voice."

Optional: a pulled-out line for emphasis works like this.

> A short, sharp line worth isolating - not a generic inspirational quote.

## Where it actually breaks / what surprised you

The strongest section is usually the one admitting where the idea fails or where you were wrong first. Avoid the obvious 5-point listicle structure and the safe conclusion everyone already agrees with.

## The takeaway

End on something specific enough that it couldn't be pasted onto a different post by swapping nouns.
